# Gatherly pitch

Hey, I am an event organizer and the vice president of a tech society. We organize events all the time, which means we are constantly looking for places to host them. But finding a venue is a hassle. We search for options, verify capacity and facilities, hunt down contacts, send the same questions again and again, wait for replies, and then compare answers scattered across different email threads. The exciting part is bringing people together; venue sourcing is the slow, repetitive work that gets in the way. That fragmented process is why I built Gatherly. Gatherly turns one plain-language event brief into an evidence-backed venue shortlist and outreach that stays under the organizer's control. Let me show you with an evening developer meetup for 200 people in Bengaluru.

Instead of hiding the work behind a loading spinner, Gatherly shows every agent handoff. The Planner turns the event brief into clear requirements, the Scout searches broadly, and the Evidence agent uses Firecrawl to gather capacity information, official pages, images, reviews, and public contact routes. The Researcher creates the shortlist and enquiry drafts, the Critic challenges weak claims, and the Verifier runs fourteen deterministic checks before the results are published. Convex persists the event and streams every stage to the interface in real time, while OpenAI powers the reasoning behind the agents.

The result is not just a list of venue names. For every option, I can see why it fits, its known capacity, amenities, contact route, images, review signals, and the source behind each important claim. If accessibility, pricing, or another requirement could not be verified, Gatherly shows it as unknown instead of inventing an answer. Even rejected candidates remain available for review, so the organizer—not the model—makes the final trade-off. Gatherly also prepares an editable email for every suitable venue, but nothing leaves the system until I review the complete message and click Send. Only then does AgentMail deliver it, with duplicate-send protection built into the workflow.

The inbox brings the venue conversations into the same workspace, with reply summaries, unanswered questions, and a suggested next message that still requires human approval. In this demo, those replies are simulated to show the complete experience, while the live product already handles research, verification, draft creation, and the first organizer-confirmed outbound email. Gatherly turns venue sourcing from scattered tabs and uncertain claims into one visible, evidence-backed process—from an event idea, to credible options, to a real conversation. The organizer keeps control, while the agents remove the repetitive work. That is Gatherly.
